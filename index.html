🏓 Who We Are

PingHub was founded in Nicosia to break table tennis out of dusty basements. We built Cyprus’s first 24/7 autonomous ping pong lounge—fusing high-level tournament infrastructure with street culture, neon aesthetics, and instant smartphone access.

Whether you want a casual midnight match with friends or professional drill masterclasses from top players Andreas Avraam and Georgia Avraam, PingHub is built for you.
